Post-quantum encrypted messaging on XDC

XDC PQOnchain encrypts messages with ML-KEM-768, the key-encapsulation standard NIST published as FIPS 203, and sends them as XDC transaction data. Keys are created and used only in your browser.

A public chain keeps data forever. “Harvest now, decrypt later” attacks store encrypted data today to crack once quantum computers mature. ML-KEM is designed to hold up against them.

Sending XDC and private messages

Message privately, sign safely, get paid and keep records.

  • XDC PQOnchain Post-quantum Web app

    Send post-quantum encrypted messages on the XDC Network. Generate an ML-KEM-768 keypair, connect MetaMask, send messages as transaction data, and decrypt anything sent to you from its transaction hash.

    Uses ML-KEM-768 (NIST FIPS 203) with AES-256-GCM. Everything runs in your browser, with no servers involved.

    Open app GitHub

  • XDC Send Offline Helper Web app

    Prepare transactions for an offline wallet and broadcast them once signed. Enter your address to get the transaction details, scan a QR code, or paste a signed transaction to send it to the network.

    Works in a desktop or mobile browser.

    Open app

  • XDC Proof Web app

    Turn an XDC payment into a PDF receipt. Enter the transaction hash, a description and optional sender and receiver details to download a verifiable proof for accounting or trade finance.

    Add your own branding to each receipt.

    Create a receipt

  • XDC ShopBot Telegram bot

    A beginner-friendly Telegram shop that sells digital products for XDC and delivers them automatically. Made for hobbyists who want to try running an online shop without advanced coding.

    Free and open source. Try the demo before setting up your own.
